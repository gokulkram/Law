## Courts and filing
- Stanley Mosk (County) Courthouse, 111 North Hill Street, Los Angeles 90012; Michael D. Antonovich Antelope Valley Courthouse, 42011 4th Street West, Lancaster 93534 (LASC Local Rules ch. 2, Rule 2.2) — https://www.lacourt.ca.gov/courtrules/CurrentCourtRulesPDF/Chap2.pdf — checked 2026-10-02
- Local Rule 2.3(a)(1)(A): every unlimited civil tort action for bodily injury, wrongful death or damage to personal property ("Personal Injury Action") must be filed in the Central District at Stanley Mosk or the North District at the Antelope Valley Courthouse — same PDF — checked 2026-10-02
- Probate: all probate proceedings filed in the Central District, except decedent's estates may be filed in the North District when the decedent resided or the petitioner resides there — same PDF (Rule 2.3) — checked 2026-10-02
- Since January 8, 2024, PI cases filed in the Central District are assigned for all purposes to Independent Calendar departments at Stanley Mosk instead of the Spring Street PI Hub — https://www.lacourt.org/newsmedia/uploads/1420231214155823NTA23NEWPROCEDURESPIHUB.pdf — checked 2026-10-02
- Court URL used in `courts`: https://www.lacourt.ca.gov/ (official court site; courthouse detail pages render only with JavaScript and could not be read) — checked 2026-10-02

## Collision reports
- LAPD: traffic collision reports can be requested online via LAPD's site; reports involving an arrest, fatality or juvenile cannot be released online and must be requested by U.S. mail — https://www.lapdonline.org/request-a-traffic-report/ — checked 2026-10-02. NOTE: direct fetch returned HTTP 403; verified from lapdonline.org search-result excerpts of that page and https://www.lapdonline.org/newsroom/lapd-introduces-obtaining-a-traffic-collision-report-online-nr16067ti/. Fee and wait time left out (sources conflicted).
- CHP: a proper party of interest may request a crash report online through the Crash Portal or with form CHP 190 at a CHP Area office — https://www.chp.ca.gov/traffic/request-a-crash-report/ — checked 2026-10-02
- CHP has primary jurisdiction for traffic enforcement and collision investigation on freeways (including within cities) and on streets/highways in unincorporated areas — https://www.chp.ca.gov/siteassets/policy/hpm/hpm-100.67/hpm-100.67-ch-3.pdf — checked 2026-10-02 (via search excerpt of the CHP manual)
- LA County Sheriff's Department provides contract law enforcement to cities in LA County (Municipal Law Enforcement Services Agreement) — https://file.lacounty.gov/SDSInter/auditor/audit_reports/1030917_2017-11-21Sheriff_sDepartment-ContractCityLawEnforcementServicesReview.pdf — checked 2026-10-02 (number of cities not used)

## Government claims
- City of Los Angeles: Claim for Damages filed online, by mail or in person with the Office of the City Clerk (200 N. Spring St., Room 395, City Hall); emailed/faxed claims not accepted; after filing, the claim transfers to the City Attorney; death/injury/personal property claims within six months — https://clerk.lacity.gov/clerk-services/council-and-public-services/claim-damage — checked 2026-10-02
- County of Los Angeles: claim mailed or delivered to the Executive Officer-Clerk of the Board of Supervisors (Attention: Claims, 500 W. Temple St., Room 383); one form per claimant; death/injury claims within 6 months — https://bos.lacounty.gov/claims-for-damages — checked 2026-10-02
- LA Metro: Claims for Damages accepted only in person or by mail; emailed claims rejected; form at https://www.metro.net/claimfordamages — https://www.metro.net/about/legal-services/ — checked 2026-10-02
- Caltrans: claims of $12,500 or less for death, personal injury or personal property may be filed directly with the Caltrans District Claims Office (form DOTLD-0274); over $12,500 go to the Government Claims Program — https://dot.ca.gov/online-services/submit-damage-claim — checked 2026-10-02

## Roads, freight and transit
- Caltrans District 7 covers Los Angeles and Ventura counties — https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-7/district-7-popular-links/d7-profile — checked 2026-10-02
- I-405 through the Sepulveda Pass is the only direct freeway connection between the San Fernando Valley and the Westside — https://www.metro.net/projects/sepulvedacorridor/ and https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-7/district-7-projects/d7-405-repave-project — checked 2026-10-02
- East Los Angeles Interchange connects I-5, I-10, US-101 and SR-60 — https://dot.ca.gov/news-releases/news-release-2023-041 and https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/safety-programs/documents/exit/calnexus-i-10-03-2020-a11y.pdf — checked 2026-10-02
- Port of Los Angeles and Port of Long Beach (San Pedro Bay port complex); port freeway network: SR-91 Artesia, I-110 Harbor, I-710 Long Beach, I-405 San Diego, SR-103/SR-47 Terminal Island Freeway; SR-47/Vincent Thomas Bridge — https://kentico.portoflosangeles.org/getmedia/52d0fb96-32ce-45d5-a336-d91ed8ac61d7/3-12-Transportation and https://portoflosangeles.org/community/traffic-interchange — checked 2026-10-02
- I-710 is the principal goods-movement connection between the Ports of LA/Long Beach terminals and the BNSF/UP rail yards in Commerce and Vernon; heavy truck traffic — https://www.metro.net/documents/2025/01/drirpdf/ — checked 2026-10-02
- Alameda Corridor: 20-mile rail cargo expressway between the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach and the rail yards near downtown Los Angeles — https://www.acta.org/ (news releases, e.g. https://www.acta.org/newsroom/news-releases/alameda-corridor-agency-authorizes-construction-on-critical-10-mile-trench/) — checked 2026-10-02
- I-5 truck-only lanes in Los Angeles County at the State Route 14 split, separating slower trucks from general traffic on the grade — https://dot.ca.gov/programs/traffic-operations/legal-truck-access/truck-only-lanes — checked 2026-10-02
- I-5 over Tejon Pass (the Grapevine) is a major corridor linking Southern California and the Central Valley; the Grapevine is in Kern County (Caltrans District 6 camera "KER-5-AT GRAPEVINE UC") — https://dot.ca.gov/news-releases/news-release-2020-002 and https://cwwp2.dot.ca.gov/vm/loc/d6/ker5atgrapevineuc.htm — checked 2026-10-02
- LA Metro operates bus and rail service in Los Angeles County — https://www.metro.net/ — checked 2026-10-02

## Death-related offices
- County of Los Angeles Department of Medical Examiner: jurisdiction over sudden, violent or unusual deaths, including deaths related to accident or injury; determines circumstances, cause and manner of death — https://me.lacounty.gov/ and https://me.lacounty.gov/for-law-enforcement-and-legal/ — checked 2026-10-02
- Autopsy/case reports: contact Medical Examiner Public Services; photographs not included and released to legal next of kin only under court order — https://me.lacounty.gov/faqs/ — checked 2026-10-02 (prices not used)
- Death certificates: LA County DPH Vital Records holds death records for the county (except Pasadena and Long Beach) for the current and previous year; others from the Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk; available 10 days after registration; in person, by mail, or online via VitalChek; authorized copies limited to listed relatives and an attorney for the estate — http://publichealth.lacounty.gov/dca/dcadeath.htm — checked 2026-10-02
