California Personal Injury Practice Areas

We represent people and families throughout California across the full range of serious injury and wrongful death matters. Whatever happened, we’ll tell you honestly how we can help.

Wrongful Death

When negligence takes a life, we help surviving families seek accountability and the compensation they’re owed. This is the heart of our practice.

Dedicated page

Car & Truck Accidents

From rear-end collisions to catastrophic commercial-truck crashes, we take on insurers and corporations to recover for medical bills, lost wages, and pain. California’s pure comparative fault rule means you can recover even if you were partly to blame.

Medical Malpractice

Misdiagnosis, surgical errors, birth injuries, and medication mistakes. We work with medical experts to prove what went wrong and who is responsible. California’s deadlines are short — generally three years from the injury or one year from discovery, whichever comes first.

Workplace Injuries

In California, workers’ compensation is usually the only remedy against an employer — so we pursue third-party claims against negligent contractors, equipment makers, and property owners.

Slip & Fall / Premises

Wet floors, broken stairs, poor security. When property owners ignore their duty to keep people safe, we hold them accountable.

Catastrophic Injury

Traumatic brain injuries, spinal cord damage, amputations, and severe burns — cases that require accounting for a lifetime of care.

Nursing Home Neglect

Our most vulnerable deserve dignity and safety. We pursue facilities that allow abuse, neglect, and preventable harm.

Defective Products

When a dangerous product causes injury, we build product-liability cases against manufacturers and distributors.

Other Injury Claims

Hurt by a government vehicle or on public property? California requires a claim within six months. Don’t see your situation? Call us. If we can’t help, we’ll point you to someone who can — at no charge.

That’s exactly what a free consultation is for. Tell us what happened — we’ll give you a straight answer.
