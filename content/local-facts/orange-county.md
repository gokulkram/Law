- CHP reports: parties of interest may request online via CHP's crash portal, or in person/by mail with form CHP 190 plus a copy of photo ID (notarized if no ID) — https://www.chp.ca.gov/traffic/request-a-crash-report ; https://www.chp.ca.gov/notify-chp/collision-report-chp-190/ (confirmed via search; direct fetch blocked) — checked 2026-10-02
- CHP increased patrols of the 73, 133, 241 and 261 Toll Roads targeting excessive speeding — https://www.thetollroads.com/news/newsroom/press-releases/transportation-corridor-agencies-and-chp-partner-to-put-the-brakes-on-excessive-speeders — checked 2026-10-02
- OC Sheriff contract cities: North Operations — Yorba Linda, Stanton, Villa Park (and north unincorporated areas); Southwest Operations — Aliso Viejo, Dana Point, Laguna Hills, Laguna Niguel, Laguna Woods, San Clemente, San Juan Capistrano; Southeast Operations — Lake Forest, Mission Viejo, Rancho Santa Margarita — https://www.ocsheriff.gov/commands-divisions/patrol-operations-command/north-operations ; https://www.ocsheriff.gov/commands-divisions/patrol-operations-command/southwest-operations/traffic-auto-theft ; https://www.ocsheriff.gov/patrol-areas/mission-viejo ; https://www.ocsheriff.gov/patrol-areas/lake-forest ; https://www.ocsheriff.gov/patrol-areas/rancho-santa-margarita ; https://www.ocsheriff.gov/news/oc-sheriff-promotes-new-commander-southeast-operations — checked 2026-10-02
- OCSD report requests: "Request Authorization for Release of Case Information" form by email, in person at Records Division in Santa Ana, or by mail/fax; call Records first to confirm availability; collision-report eligibility (drivers, owners, parents of minor driver, passengers listed, insurers…) — https://www.ocsheriff.gov/commands-divisions/professional-services-command/records/information-management/report-request — checked 2026-10-02

## Public-entity claims
- County of Orange "Claim for Money or Damages Against the County of Orange" form (Revised 1/24) is on the Clerk of the Board forms page; mailed or delivered to Clerk of the Board of Supervisors, 400 W. Civic Center Drive, 6th Floor, Santa Ana, CA 92701; injury/personal property claims within 6 months — https://cob.oc.gov/forms ; https://cob.oc.gov/sites/cob/files/2024-01/Revised%20Claim%20Form%202024.pdf — checked 2026-10-02
- City of Irvine Claim for Damages (Form 10-04), City Clerk, online submission available — https://www.cityofirvine.gov/content/claim-damages — checked 2026-10-02
- City of Santa Ana: claim delivered in person or mailed to City Clerk; original wet signature; no electronic service; late claims need an Application for Leave to Present a Late Claim — https://santa-ana.gov/claims/ — checked 2026-10-02
- City of Anaheim: original claim to the Office of the City Clerk, forwarded to Risk Management — https://www.anaheim.net/438/File-a-Claim — checked 2026-10-02
- OCTA Claim for Damage: within 6 months of accident; for vehicle damage only the registered owner may present/sign; mail to OCTA Risk Management (P.O. box in Orange); Medicare reporting form for bodily injury claims — https://www.octa.net/contact/contact-us/claim-form/ — checked 2026-10-02
- OCTA operates OC Bus — https://octa.net/ — checked 2026-10-02
- State claims: DGS Government Claims Program (online filing, $25 fee); page directs claims for potholes/damage on CA highways to Caltrans — https://www.dgs.ca.gov/en/ORIM/File-A-Claim — checked 2026-10-02

## Roads, agencies and freight
- Freeway names: Santa Ana Freeway (I-5), San Diego Freeway (I-405), Costa Mesa Freeway (SR-55), Orange Freeway (SR-57), Riverside Freeway (SR-91), Garden Grove Freeway (SR-22), San Gabriel River Freeway (I-605) — https://www.octa.net/programs-projects/projects/freeway-projects/overview — checked 2026-10-02
- Caltrans District 12 is Orange County — https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-12 — checked 2026-10-02
- I-405 Improvement Project (OCTA with Caltrans): regular lane added each direction Euclid St to I-605; 405 Express Lanes between SR-73 in Costa Mesa and I-605 at the county line — https://www.octa.net/programs-projects/projects/freeway-projects/i-405-improvements-project/overview — checked 2026-10-02
- I-5 Improvement Project (I-405 to SR-55), OCTA with Caltrans, in Irvine and Tustin; adds a general-purpose lane each direction; construction 2026–2029 — https://www.octa.net/programs-projects/projects/freeway-projects/i-5-i-405-to-sr-55/overview — checked 2026-10-02
- SR-91 Improvement Project between SR-55 and SR-57 incl. SR-91/SR-57 interchange; cities Anaheim, Orange, Placentia, Fullerton, Yorba Linda — https://www.octa.net/news/news-releases/sr-91-improvement-project-gets-underway-in-oc/ — checked 2026-10-02
- 91 Express Lanes: toll road in the median of SR-91 between the SR-55/SR-91 interchange and I-15; OCTA owns the Orange County segment (franchise acquired 2003) — https://octa.net/getting-around/express-lanes/91-express-lanes/about — checked 2026-10-02
- Toll Roads 73, 133, 241, 261 are owned by the State of California and operated by the Transportation Corridor Agencies (two JPAs formed 1986) — https://thetollroads.com/help/how-can-we-help-you/frequently-asked-questions/who-owns-and-operates-the-toll-roads/ ; https://www.thetollroads.com/about/about-tca/what-is-tca — checked 2026-10-02
- SR-91 is one of the main routes from the Port of Los Angeles / Port of Long Beach to warehouse and distribution uses in the Inland Empire; Orange County is near the nationally significant San Pedro Bay ports and the Inland Empire import hub; rail-truck intermodal facilities in Orange, Buena Park and Anaheim; warehousing clusters along the SR-91 corridor (corridor cities La Palma, Buena Park, Fullerton, Anaheim, Placentia, Yorba Linda); SR-91 and SR-55 (between SR-91 and I-405 in Costa Mesa) are part of the STAA National Network — OCTA/Caltrans 2022 SR-91 Comprehensive Multimodal Corridor Plan, https://www.octa.net/pdf/OCTA_91_CMCP.pdf — checked 2026-10-02

## Coroner, death certificates
- OC Coroner is a division of the Sheriff's Department (Sheriff-Coroner); investigates all unnatural and unexpected deaths or where the attending doctor can't state cause; records released only after case closed; email requests returned by email at no charge, in-person per-page fee; pending cases ~4–6 months; Coroner doesn't issue death certificates but provides Verification of Death letter; funeral director typically orders certificates; personal belongings released to legal next of kin by appointment — https://ocsheriff.gov/commands-divisions/investigations-special-operations-command/coroner/faqs — checked 2026-10-02
- Coroner Division facility in Santa Ana (1071 W. Santa Ana Blvd.) — https://ocsheriff.gov/location/coroner-division (search result text) — checked 2026-10-02
- Death certificates: within first 30 days from the Health Care Agency's Office of Vital Records; after 30 days from the Clerk-Recorder — https://www.ocgov.com/services/certificates-registration-licenses/death-certificates ; https://www.ochealthinfo.com/ovr ; https://ocrecorder.com/clerk-services/vital-records — checked 2026-10-02
