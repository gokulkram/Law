- North County Regional Center, 325 South Melrose Drive, Vista, CA 92081 — "a full service branch court"; hears criminal, civil, family and other matters — https://www.sdcourt.ca.gov/sdcourt/generalinformation/courtlocations/northcountycourthouse — checked 2026-10-02
- Civil Business Office locations: Hall of Justice (Room 225) and North County Regional Center accept civil filings; East County (El Cajon) and South County (Chula Vista) business offices are "restraining orders only" — https://www.sdcourt.ca.gov/sdcourt/civil2/civillocations — checked 2026-10-02

## Collision reports
- SDPD responds only to injury accidents and hit-and-runs with injuries or suspect/lead information; property-damage crashes: drivers exchange listed information; freeway incidents are reported to CHP — https://www.sandiego.gov/police/services/traffic-accidents-reports-tickets — checked 2026-10-02
- SDPD reports: allow 3–10 business days; request online through LexisNexis eCrash, or in person at SDPD Records Division, 1401 Broadway, San Diego 92101; drivers, injured passengers, insurers and employers (if driver is an employee) can get a copy — https://www.sandiego.gov/police/services/get-police-report — checked 2026-10-02 (the page cites a Vehicle Code section not on FACTS.md; not cited in content)
- CHP crash reports: proper Party of Interest may request online through the Crash Portal, or in person/by mail at a CHP Area office with form CHP 190 — https://www.chp.ca.gov/traffic/request-a-crash-report — checked 2026-10-02
- CHP Border Division area offices in San Diego County include San Diego (645), Oceanside (650), El Cajon (680), plus Otay Mesa (646), San Onofre (651) and Rainbow (686) commercial vehicle enforcement facilities — https://www.chp.ca.gov/find-an-office/border-division — checked 2026-10-02
- Border Division "is also home to the largest U.S. Armed Services population within the country" (used only as "a large military community") — https://www.chp.ca.gov/find-an-office/border-division — checked 2026-10-02
- CHP San Diego Area: freeways in the cities of San Diego, Chula Vista, National City and Coronado, plus unincorporated Bonita, Otay Mesa, Lincoln Acres and 4S Ranch — https://www.chp.ca.gov/find-an-office/border-division/645-san-diego/ — checked 2026-10-02
- CHP Oceanside Area: patrols I-5, SR-78, I-15, unincorporated SR-76 and county roads in Rancho Santa Fe, Bonsall, Fallbrook, Valley Center and Palomar Mountain — https://www.chp.ca.gov/find-an-office/border-division/650-oceanside/ — checked 2026-10-02
- CHP El Cajon Area: major freeways I-8, SR-125, SR-52, SR-67, SR-78, SR-94, SR-54, SR-188 and SR-79; unincorporated county roads in East County — https://www.chp.ca.gov/find-an-office/border-division/680-el-cajon/ — checked 2026-10-02

## Truck / freight / border
- Otay Mesa CVEF: "port-of-entry" commercial vehicle enforcement facility in Otay Mesa for commercial vehicles entering California from Mexico; oversees the Tecate CVEF; inspections of equipment, loading, driver qualifications incl. sobriety and fatigue — https://www.chp.ca.gov/find-an-office/border-division/646-otay-mesa-commercial-vehicle-enforcement-facility/ — checked 2026-10-02
- San Onofre inspection facility on I-5 in northern San Diego County, inspects northbound and southbound commercial vehicles — https://www.chp.ca.gov/find-an-office/border-division/651-san-onofre-commercial-vehicle-enforcement-facility/ — checked 2026-10-02
- Rainbow CVEF on I-15 at Rainbow Valley Blvd. West (Fallbrook), northbound and southbound facilities — https://www.chp.ca.gov/find-an-office/border-division/686-rainbow-commercial-vehicle-enforcement-facility/ — checked 2026-10-02
- 1994: federal government closed the San Ysidro POE to commercial vehicles, rerouting them to Otay Mesa POE about six miles east; SR-905 (six lanes, opened 2012) begins less than a mile west of I-5 and runs east to the Otay Mesa POE; SR-905/SR-125/SR-11 connectors give commercial and passenger vehicles leaving Otay Mesa direct highway access — SANDAG/Caltrans fact sheet: https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/district-11/documents/201906-d11-piofactsheet905sr125sr11-a11y.pdf — checked 2026-10-02
- I-805/SR-905 connectors exist (Caltrans District 11 closure notices) — https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-11/news/20260814-sr905i805closures — checked 2026-10-02
- SR-11 / Otay Mesa East Port of Entry: construction complete on SR-11, SR-125 and SR-905 leading to the site; port of entry under construction (no opening date used) — https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-11/current-projects/sr11 — checked 2026-10-02
- Tecate is a port of entry in the CBP San Diego Field Office — https://www.cbp.gov/about/contact/ports/CA — checked 2026-10-02
- Port of San Diego operates two marine cargo terminals: Tenth Avenue Marine Terminal (San Diego; refrigerated, bulk, breakbulk; TAMT Barrio Logan truck route map) and National City Marine Terminal (National City waterfront, south end of San Diego Bay; vehicle import/export) — https://www.portofsandiego.org/maritime/terminals — checked 2026-10-02
- Caltrans District 11 covers San Diego and Imperial counties — https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-11 — checked 2026-10-02

## Government claims
- City of San Diego: Risk Management Department, Public Liability and Loss Recovery Division; most claims within six months; Public Liability Claims Portal or paper "Claim Against the City of San Diego Form (RM-9)"; email first-notice claims no longer accepted since Dec 11, 2023 — https://www.sandiego.gov/riskmanagement/services/liability — checked 2026-10-02
- County of San Diego: Office of County Counsel, Claims and Investigation Division (1600 Pacific Highway, Room 335); claims by U.S. mail and in person; Claim Form CD-1 (no form required, but provided); no fax filings — https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/CountyCounsel/claims.html — checked 2026-10-02
- State of California (incl. Caltrans): DGS Office of Risk and Insurance Management, Government Claims Program; online or paper claim; 6 months for death/injury/personal property — https://www.dgs.ca.gov/ORIM/File-a-Claim — checked 2026-10-02 (fee amount not used)

## Wrongful death — county offices
- San Diego County Department of the Medical Examiner, 5570 Overland Avenue, Suite 101, San Diego, CA 92123 (content says only "on Overland Avenue in San Diego") — https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/me/office.html — checked 2026-10-02
- ME reports: request by email to records unit with decedent's name, date of death, date of birth, ME case number; available only after the case closes (average several months — numbers not used); no preliminary or draft reports; reports are public record unless sealed by law enforcement; emailed copies free, hard copies per-page fee; ME does not issue death certificates (contact Office of Vital Records) — https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/me/copies.html — checked 2026-10-02
- Death certificates: deaths within the last 2 years — Office of Vital Records and Statistics (HHSA); more than 2 years ago — San Diego Recorder/Clerk; Authorized Certified Copy only to authorized individuals with notarized sworn statement; Informational Certified Copy to all others — https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/hhsa/programs/phs/office_of_vital_records_and_statistics/death_certificates.html — checked 2026-10-02
