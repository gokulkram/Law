## Roads, freight, Caltrans
- Caltrans District 8 covers Riverside and San Bernardino counties; distribution centers growing in the Ontario/I-15 corridor bring increased truck traffic; truck lanes becoming a necessity on major routes; wind gusts on I-15 in the Cajon Pass frequently cause high-profile vehicles to be diverted or stopped when gusts reach 55 mph — https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-8/district-8-popular-links/about-caltrans-district-8 (fetched) — checked 2026-10-02
- SR-60 runs to the 60/91/215 interchange in Riverside County; SR-91 in the City of Riverside; I-215 in Perris — https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-8/district-8-current-projects/sr-60-pavement-rehab ; https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-8/district-8-current-projects/sr-91-pavement-rehab-riverside ; https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-8/district-8-current-projects/interstate-215-pavement-repair (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- I-10 and I-15 in District 8 — https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-8/district-8-popular-links/about-caltrans-district-8 (fetched) — checked 2026-10-02
- Larger claims against Caltrans go to the State Government Claims Program — https://dot.ca.gov/online-services/submit-damage-claim (fetched) — checked 2026-10-02
- CHP has traffic jurisdiction in unincorporated areas and on freeways — https://www.chp.ca.gov/siteassets/policy/hpm/hpm-100.67/hpm-100.67-ch-3.pdf (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- CHP collision reports requested with form CHP 190 — https://www.chp.ca.gov/notify-chp/collision-report-chp-190/ (search) — checked 2026-10-02

## Collision reports — Riverside Police Department
- Reports available about 10 working days after the incident; online via LexisNexis eCrash, in person at 10540 Magnolia Ave. Ste B, or by mail; fatality / on-duty emergency vehicle reports only through the department — https://riversideca.gov/rpd/resources-forms/permit/get-copy-traffic-collision-report (search) — checked 2026-10-02

## Government claims and transit
- County of Riverside: claim must be in writing, form optional; signed hard copy mailed or presented to Clerk of the Board of Supervisors, Attn: Claims Division, 4080 Lemon Street; electronic/fax not accepted — https://rc-hr.com/risk-management/liability-claims (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- County of San Bernardino: "Claim Against County of San Bernardino" form, send to address on the form; Liability Division — https://rm.sbcounty.gov/liability-claims/ (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- City of Riverside: Claim for Damages online, in person or by mail via the City Clerk; injury claims within six months — https://riversideca.gov/cityclerk/online-documents/claims-damages (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- Riverside Transit Agency: transit agency for western Riverside County; member cities include Corona, Eastvale, Hemet, Jurupa Valley, Lake Elsinore, Menifee, Moreno Valley, Murrieta, Norco, Perris, Riverside, Temecula — https://www.riversidetransit.com/index.php/about-rta/who-we-are (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- Omnitrans: public transit agency for the San Bernardino Valley; cities include Chino, Colton, Fontana, Loma Linda, Ontario, Rancho Cucamonga, Redlands, Rialto, San Bernardino, Upland — https://omnitrans.org/about/about-omnitrans/ (search) — checked 2026-10-02
