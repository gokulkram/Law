Our Story

Big-firm results, with the care of people who know you

West Stone Law was founded by trial attorneys who grew tired of watching insurance companies take advantage of people at their lowest moments. We set out to build something different: a firm with the resources to take on any opponent, and the heart to treat every client like family.

Today, we focus our practice on personal injury and wrongful death — because that focus is what produces results. We don’t dabble. We do this every day, and we do it well.
