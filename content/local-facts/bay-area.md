# Local facts — Bay Area (/bay-area)

Each fact used in `content/locations/bay-area.ts`, with its official source. All checked 2026-10-02.
Note: several sites block automated fetches (HTTP 403); where marked "search", the fact was confirmed from the
official domain's own page text as returned by a domain-restricted web search, not a direct fetch.

## Courts
- San Francisco Superior Court Civil Division is at the Civic Center Courthouse, 400 McAllister Street (Room 103), San Francisco, CA 94102; it handles civil trials and filings — https://sf.courts.ca.gov/divisions/civil-division (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- Alameda Superior Court, René C. Davidson Courthouse, 1225 Fallon Street, Oakland, CA 94612; civil case filings are presented there (Room 109); civil direct calendar heard there — https://www.alameda.courts.ca.gov/locations ; http://www.alameda.courts.ca.gov/Pages.aspx/Oakland-Ren-C-Davidson-Courthouse (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- San Mateo Superior Court, Southern Branch: Hall of Justice and Records, 400 County Center, Redwood City, CA 94063; Civil Division clerk's office (civil trials, filings) — https://sanmateo.courts.ca.gov/location/southern-branch-hall-justice-and-records ; https://sanmateo.courts.ca.gov/divisions/civil-division (search) — checked 2026-10-02

## Roads, freight, Caltrans
- I-80 crosses the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge; Caltrans schedules overnight lane closures for maintenance/rehabilitation — https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-4/d4-news/2026-03-04-sfobb-west-span-rehabilitation-project ; https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-4/d4-news/2026-08-07-i80-sfobb-overnight-lane-closures (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- Caltrans District 4 is based in Oakland and serves the nine Bay Area counties (Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, Sonoma) — https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-4/d4-programs/d4-public-affairs (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- Oakland seaport is bounded by I-80 (north) and I-880 (east); the Port lists I-80, I-580 and I-880 as freight routes serving it — https://www.portofoakland.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Narrative_PortOakland_PIDP_20240510.pdf ; https://www.portofoakland.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Clean-Ports-Project-Narrative_Port-of-Oakland_Final.pdf (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- Larger claims against Caltrans go to the State Government Claims Program (DGS); smaller ones to the Caltrans district claims office — https://dot.ca.gov/online-services/submit-damage-claim (fetched) — checked 2026-10-02
- CHP has traffic jurisdiction in unincorporated areas and on freeways (excluded only from non-freeway state highways inside cities) — https://www.chp.ca.gov/siteassets/policy/hpm/hpm-100.67/hpm-100.67-ch-3.pdf (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- CHP collision reports are requested with form CHP 190 from the CHP office that reported the collision — https://www.chp.ca.gov/notify-chp/collision-report-chp-190/ (search) — checked 2026-10-02

## Collision reports — SFPD
- SFPD prepares traffic collision reports only for hit-and-run, drunk driving and personal injury cases; allow at least five business days; request online, by mail, or in person (Report Management Section, 1245 3rd Street) — https://www.sanfranciscopolice.org/traffic-collision-report ; https://www.sanfranciscopolice.org/get-service/police-reports (search) — checked 2026-10-02

## Transit agencies and government claims
- Muni is the transit division of the SFMTA (buses, light rail, historic streetcars, cable cars) — https://www.sfmta.com/muni-transit (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- Claims against the City and County of San Francisco (incl. transit injuries): form on City Attorney's page; original mailed or hand-delivered to Controller's Office Claims Division; no email/fax; injury claims within 6 months; for bus/light rail, give line and vehicle number — https://www.sf.gov/cityattorney-file-claim (search; sfcityattorney.org/claims/ now redirects here) — checked 2026-10-02
- BART (San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit District) provides an Insurance Claim Form for claims against the District — https://www.bart.gov/about/business/insurance (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- AC Transit: Claim for Damages form filed with the District Secretary (1600 Franklin Street, Oakland); original signed form delivered or mailed; fax/email not accepted; covers bus incidents and falls on District property — https://www.actransit.org/government-tort-claims (search) — checked 2026-10-02

## Areas served
- AC Transit service-area cities include Alameda, Berkeley, Emeryville, Fremont, Hayward, Newark, Oakland, San Leandro (Fremont and Newark in southwestern Alameda County) — https://www.actransit.org/resources-for-cities ; https://www.actransit.org/maps-schedules (search) — checked 2026-10-02
- San Mateo County incorporated cities include Burlingame, Daly City, Millbrae, Pacifica, Redwood City, San Bruno, San Mateo, South San Francisco — https://www.smcgov.org/lafco/cities-san-mateo-county (search) — checked 2026-10-02
