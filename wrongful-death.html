Wrongful Death Attorneys

When negligence takes a life, the law gives surviving families the right to seek accountability and compensation. We are here to help you carry that fight.

Nothing can undo the loss of someone you love. But holding the responsible party accountable can bring a measure of justice — and the financial stability your family needs to move forward.

What is a wrongful death claim?

A wrongful death claim is a civil action brought when a person dies because of the negligent, reckless, or intentional act of another. Unlike a criminal case — which punishes wrongdoing — a wrongful death claim is brought by the surviving family to recover for their losses.

These claims commonly arise from:

  • Car, truck, and motorcycle collisions
  • Medical malpractice and hospital negligence
  • Workplace and construction accidents
  • Defective products and dangerous premises
  • Nursing home neglect and abuse

Who can file a claim?

Eligibility varies by state, but those entitled to bring a claim typically include a spouse, children, parents, or the personal representative of the deceased person’s estate. We’ll help you understand exactly who has standing in your situation — and the deadlines that apply.

What compensation may be available?

While every case is different, wrongful death recoveries can include:

  • Economic losses — lost income and benefits the deceased would have provided, plus medical and funeral expenses
  • Loss of companionship — the guidance, care, and relationship the family has lost
  • Conscious pain and suffering — through a related survival action, where applicable
  • Punitive damages — in cases of especially egregious conduct

Act before time runs out

Every state imposes a statute of limitations — a strict deadline for filing. Evidence also fades quickly: vehicles are repaired, records are lost, and memories blur. The sooner we begin, the stronger your case. There is no cost to speak with us, and we never charge a fee unless we win.
